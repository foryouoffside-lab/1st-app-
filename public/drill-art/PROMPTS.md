# Drill art — image generation prompts

Ten prompts, one per drill. Same house style so the ten cards read as one set.
Paste the **STYLE** block + one **SUBJECT** line into your image tool. Export
**4:3**, 1024×768 or larger, save as `<id>.webp` in this folder.

---

## STYLE (prepend to every prompt)

> Premium mobile game key art. Near-black background (#050508). One bold hero
> object, centered, floating. Cinematic volumetric lighting, soft glow, faint
> drifting particle dust, a subtle grid floor fading to pure black. Minimal,
> high-contrast, sharp focus, 3D octane-render look. No text, no letters, no
> logos, no UI, no watermark. 4:3 aspect ratio.

Keep the hero object **centered** — the card overlays a badge top-left and a
play button + timer along the bottom.

---

## SUBJECTS

**quick-dodge** — accent hot pink (#ec4899)
> A glowing white sphere weaving at speed between tall angular hot-pink neon
> obstacle bars, long motion-blur light trails behind it, a feeling of a
> split-second dodge.

**distraction-fighter** — accent cyan (#06b6d4)
> A single calm solid cyan cube in razor-sharp focus at the center, ringed by a
> faint hexagonal shield of light holding back a chaotic blurred swarm of small
> multicoloured shapes pressing in from all sides.

**card-matching** — accent blue (#3b82f6)
> Two identical glowing blue rounded tiles floating and flipping face-up in
> mid-air with a soft thread of light connecting them, rows of dim face-down
> tiles blurred in the background.

**multi-tasking** — accent violet (#8e61f6)
> A screen split down the middle by a single tall glowing violet light-seam.
> From that seam, two streams of small wireframe geometric glyphs (triangle,
> circle, square, star, diamond, hexagon) flow outward in opposite directions
> — left stream drifting left, right stream drifting right — each with faint
> motion-blur trails. One glyph in the left stream and a DIFFERENT glyph in the
> right stream glow bright violet as the two things being tracked at once.
> Symmetrical, calm, a sense of attention held in two places. Landscape.

**moving-target** — accent cyan (#06b6d4)
> A glowing cyan targeting reticle locked onto a bright darting orb, a long
> curved light trail arcing across the frame showing the orb's path.

**grid-memorization** — accent blue (#3b82f6)
> A floating 4x4 grid of dark glass cells, five of them lit bright blue, two of
> the lit cells breaking apart into rising particles as if fading from memory.

**shade-finder** — accent cyan (#06b6d4)
> A tight grid of nearly identical soft cyan-teal squares with exactly one
> square a barely-brighter shade, a faint lens glint catching that one square.

**tower-of-hanoi** — accent amber (#f59e0b)
> Three slim vertical pegs holding stacks of glowing amber rings of decreasing
> size, one ring lifted mid-move along a clean arc of light, tidy isometric
> view.

**concentration-grid** — accent cyan (#06b6d4)
> A 5x5 grid of small glowing cyan numerals on dark tiles, a bright horizontal
> scan-line sweeping across the grid, one tile lit brighter than the rest.

**finger-sequencing** — accent hot pink (#ec4899)
> A constellation of glowing hot-pink nodes joined by thin light lines into a
> single winding numbered path, one node pressed and pulsing with a ripple of
> light.
